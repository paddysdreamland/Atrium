Soundscape Player

Information

Half-Life 2's soundscapes, played back in the browser by a port of the Source engine's own soundscape system. These are the ambient beds, loops and one-off sounds the game layers around you in every area. Here's how it works:

  • Click the title in the display to pick a soundscape and hit Play. Picking another one while playing crossfades to it, the same way the game does when you walk into a new area.
  • The radar shows the sounds around you: loops glow at their positions, random sounds ripple where they play, and ambient sounds surround you. Drag the numbered positions around to move their sounds, snapping to the grid if you like. Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan, and double-click to reset the view. The cog holds the room effect and fade time settings.
  • Every loop and random sound gets its own row below, with a live meter, Mute and Solo.

The soundscape logic follows the Source SDK 2013 code closely. The engine's own mixing isn't part of the SDK, so distance falloff, panning and the room effects are close approximations rather than exact copies.

Credits

Half-Life 2, its soundscapes and its sounds are made by Valve. The soundscape system is ported from Valve's Source SDK 2013.

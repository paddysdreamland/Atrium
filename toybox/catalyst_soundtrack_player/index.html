How it works

Mirror's Edge™ Catalyst's soundtrack, made dynamic, entirely within the browser. Every soundtrack is split into four channels of two layers each, which you can mix, swap between tracks, and blend together.

  • Click the title to pick a main soundtrack, which fills in every channel. Swap individual channels to other tracks for more variety, or hit Shuffle in the display's corner to have it done for you.
  • Hit play and enjoy listening. Each channel has its own fader and On, Solo and Lock buttons (Lock keeps Dynamic Layer Handling away from it), and the meters show what every layer is actually playing.
  • Use the arrow next to a channel's name to pick which layers it plays, so two channels can both be percussion from different tracks, for example.
  • No refreshing needed: change the soundtrack or any channel while playing and hit Transition to crossfade to it on the beat. The cog holds the playback options.
  • Download the 8-channel version to replace the soundtracks in Mirror's Edge™ Catalyst, or a stereo mixdown of exactly what you hear.
